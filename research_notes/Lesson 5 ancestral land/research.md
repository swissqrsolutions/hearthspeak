## 0. Story skeleton facts (quick reference)

- **Setting:** a village in Maddur taluk, Mandya district. Maddur town sits on the Bengaluru–Mysuru highway and is famous for **Maddur vade**. Nearby are the **Kokkare Bellur** bird village (painted storks and pelicans, about 12 km from Maddur [V]), the Shimsha river, and the Hoysala-era **Ugra Narasimha temple** in Maddur town [V].
- **Expressway:** the 10-lane Bengaluru–Mysuru expressway (NH-275, about 118 km) was inaugurated in March 2023 [K]. It has a Maddur bypass, and one source mentions a toll plaza near Maddur [V].
- **Sugar factories:** the state-owned **Mysugar** (Mysore Sugar Co., Mandya city, founded 1933) [K]. A private mill, **Chamundeshwari Sugars**, is at K.M. Doddi/Bharathinagar in Maddur taluk [K/?]. Jaggery units are called *ಆಲೆಮನೆ* (*ālemane*).
- **Dairy:** milk to the village dairy co-op and on to **MANMUL** (Mandya Milk Union) is many families' steady cash income [K]. The native cattle breed is **Hallikar** [K].
- **Land units:** 1 acre (ಎಕರೆ) = 40 **guntas** (ಗುಂಟೆ). 1 gunta ≈ 1,089 sq ft ≈ 101 m² [K]. A realistic inheritance would be 4–8 acres split over 2–3 survey numbers (e.g. Sy. No. 112/2 wetland and 87/1 dryland).
- **Wet vs dry land:** *ನೀರಾವರಿ / ಗದ್ದೆ* (canal-irrigated wetland, for paddy and cane), *ಖುಷ್ಕಿ / ಹೊಲ* (rain-fed dryland, for ragi, avare, horse gram), *ತೋಟ* (garden land: coconut, areca, mulberry). These labels appear on the RTC and change the land's value a lot.

## 1. Law and procedure (2025–26)

### 1.1 Hindu Succession Act, 1956 (HSA)
- **Self-acquired property:** if the grandfather dies **intestate** (without a will), it goes under **Section 8** in equal shares to his **Class I heirs**: widow, sons, daughters, mother, and the children of any predeceased child [K]. Daughters, including married daughters, get exactly the same share as sons [K].
- **Ancestral / coparcenary property** (inherited through the male line, traditionally up to 4 generations, never divided) [K]. Under **Section 6 as amended in 2005**, in force from **9 Sept 2005**, a daughter is a **coparcener by birth**, in the same way as a son [V].
- **Vineeta Sharma v. Rakesh Sharma** (Supreme Court, 3-judge bench, **11 Aug 2020**) [V]:
  - The daughter's right is "retroactive": it arises from birth, and the father need **not** have been alive on 9-9-2005.
  - Partitions completed before **20 Dec 2004** stand only if they were made by a **registered partition deed or a court decree**.
  - A plea of **oral partition** is generally not accepted unless it is backed by contemporaneous public documents [K, from the judgment's widely reported holding].
- **Karnataka nuance:** Karnataka amended the HSA earlier, with **Sections 6A/6B**. The key date discussed is **30-07-1994**, from which unmarried daughters became coparceners [V: Karnataka Judicial Academy paper index; details ?].
- **Later Karnataka HC ruling:** *Leela Scottish Lace v. Vinoda* (2026) held that the coparcenary right cannot vest in a daughter who **died before** the 2005 amendment came into force [V]. This could become a plot point if one aunt died before 2005 and her children claim her share.
- **Property inherited after 1956 under Section 8** is generally treated as the heir's **separate** property, not ancestral, in the next generation [K; SC Yudhishter 1986, Uttam 2022]. This "is it really ancestral?" fight is the classic courtroom argument.
- **Wills:** a will needs no registration but must be attested by two witnesses [K]. An unregistered "will" produced at the last minute by one sibling is a realistic plot twist.
- **Women's shares are often denied in practice:** Revenue Minister Krishna Byre Gowda has publicly criticised kin denying women their land share [V, DH]. Sisters are often pressured to sign a **release deed** (*ಹಕ್ಕು ಬಿಡುಗಡೆ ಪತ್ರ*) "for the family".

### 1.2 Three ways to partition
1. **Oral family arrangement** (*ಬಾಯ್ಮಾತಿನ ಪಾಲು*), sometimes with a written **memorandum of family settlement**. A memorandum that records a past oral partition does not need registration (Kale v. Dy. Director of Consolidation, SC 1976) [K]. It is cheap, but fragile, and it often fails in revenue offices.
2. **Registered partition deed** (*ವಿಭಾಗ ಪತ್ರ / ಪಾಲು ಪತ್ರ*) at the **Sub-Registrar** (Maddur has a Sub-Registrar office [K]), through the **Kaveri 2.0** portal.
   - **Stamp duty** is a fixed sum per share, but sources conflict [?]:
     - about ₹1,000 per share in municipal areas;
     - for agricultural land, previously ₹250 per share, reportedly rising to ₹1,000 per share in gram panchayat areas (housing.com).
   - **Registration fee** went up from 1% to 2% for documents executed after **31 Aug 2025** [V]. It is unclear whether this also applies to the fixed-fee partition instruments [?].
   - **Digital e-stamping** (Karnataka Stamp (Digital e-Stamp) Rules, 2025) has been operational since about Jan 2026 [V].
   - **Advice for the lesson:** say "the stamp duty is a small fixed amount per share; the registration fee and the lawyer's fee are the bigger costs." Avoid quoting an exact number.
3. **Court partition suit** (*ವಿಭಾಗದ ದಾವೆ*), an **O.S.** (Original Suit) in the Civil Judge court [K]. Maddur has a Civil Judge & JMFC court [K/?]. The stages are:
   - plaint, then summons, then written statement, then issues, then evidence, then **preliminary decree**, which declares the shares;
   - **Final Decree Proceedings (FDP)**: a court **commissioner** (often a surveyor) divides the land by metes and bounds;
   - **Temporary injunction** (*ತಡೆಯಾಜ್ಞೆ / stay*) under Order 39 CPC is commonly sought to stop a sibling from selling or building.
   - **Court fee:** under the Karnataka Court Fees & Suits Valuation Act **s.35(2)**, a plaintiff who pleads **joint possession** pays a small fixed fee (₹200 in reported cases). Under **s.35(1)**, a plaintiff who was **ousted** pays ad valorem on the value of their share [V, case summaries]. Siblings who live in Bengaluru often fight over this point.
   - **Timelines:** in **Oct 2025** the Karnataka HC said partition suits "occupy the top of the list" for delay and "often take decades". It urged CPC reform of the final-decree stage (*Veerabhadrappa v. Channappa Gowda*) [V]. Karnataka district courts had about **1.61 lakh partition suits** pending (data to 2023) [V].
   - **Realistic story figure:** 3–7 years to a preliminary decree, plus years more for the final decree. Lawyer fees in a taluk court are perhaps ₹30,000 to ₹1.5 lakh+ over the life of the case [?, estimate, not sourced].

### 1.3 Mutation (ಖಾತೆ ಬದಲಾವಣೆ / ಪೌತಿ ಖಾತೆ) after death, in the RTC (ಪಹಣಿ)
- **RTC** (Record of Rights, Tenancy & Crops) is commonly called **Pahani** (*ಪಹಣಿ*). It is online on **Bhoomi** (landrecords.karnataka.gov.in) [V].
- **Mutation is not proof of title.** It is a fiscal/revenue record, a point courts repeat often [V].
- **Inheritance mutation is called *pauti/pouthi khata*** [V]. The process [K, with details from V sources]:
  1. **Death certificate** (*ಮರಣ ಪ್ರಮಾಣ ಪತ್ರ*). In rural areas it comes from the registrar of births and deaths; for village deaths this has traditionally been the Village Accountant or the GP [?].
  2. **Family tree / survivorship certificate** (*ವಂಶವೃಕ್ಷ ಪ್ರಮಾಣ ಪತ್ರ*). Apply at the **Nadakacheri** (Atalji Janasnehi Kendra) at the hobli level; it is issued by the **Tahsildar** after a local enquiry. One source cites **14 working days** under the **Sakala** service guarantee [V].
  3. **Pauti application** with heirs' Aadhaar. The **Village Accountant** (*ಗ್ರಾಮ ಲೆಕ್ಕಿಗ*) does the spot enquiry and records statements (*ಮಹಜರ್*). The post is now officially called **Grama Adalitha Adhikari / Village Administrative Officer** (*ಗ್ರಾಮ ಆಡಳಿತ ಅಧಿಕಾರಿ*), though villagers still say "VA" or "ಶಾನುಭೋಗರು" (old term) [K/?].
  4. **Notice to all heirs** (Karnataka Land Revenue Act **s.128–129**: duty to report, mutation register, objection period) [K]. Sources disagree on the timing: one says about **30-day notice**, another describes faster auto-mutation [? conflicting].
  5. If there are no objections, the **Revenue Inspector** (*ಕಂದಾಯ ನಿರೀಕ್ಷಕ*, RI) certifies the entry. If an heir objects, it becomes a **disputed case before the Tahsildar** (an "RRT" dispute) [K]. Appeals go to the **Assistant Commissioner** (s.136(2)), then revision to the **Deputy Commissioner** [K].
- **State drive:** Karnataka's **e-Pauthi** campaign is a mission-mode drive. Aadhaar seeding found roughly **48–51 lakh RTCs in the names of dead owners** (figures vary by report) [V, Hans India / DH / South First]. Officials are told to do site inspections and create heirs' accounts from family trees [V].
- **Practical frictions** (good dialogue material) [K]:
  - Aadhaar is not linked to the RTC.
  - "The server is down".
  - The tree is missing a daughter's name.
  - A sibling living in the US needs a notarised/apostilled consent or a **power of attorney** (*ಜಿಪಿಎ / ಮುಖ್ತಾರ್ ನಾಮೆ*).
  - Old RTCs still carry the great-grandfather's name, so a chain of pauti entries is needed.
- **Succession certificate** (*ಉತ್ತರಾಧಿಕಾರ ಪ್ರಮಾಣ ಪತ್ರ*, Indian Succession Act s.372, issued by a civil court) is for **debts, bank deposits and securities**, **not** for land [V]. It fits the story if Thatha's FD or co-op society shares are frozen. The **legal-heir certificate** is a separate revenue document. In Karnataka the family-tree certificate usually does this job [K/?].
- **Other documents** [K]:
  - **EC** (Encumbrance Certificate, *ಋಣಭಾರ ಪ್ರಮಾಣ ಪತ್ರ*) from the Sub-Registrar on Kaveri.
  - **MR** (Mutation Register extract).
  - **Akarband** (survey area statement).
  - **Tippani / Atlas** survey records.
  - **11E sketch** (needed before any sale or partition of part of a survey number).
  - **Podi (phodi) durasti**: subdividing a survey number into **hissa** numbers (e.g. 112/2A, 112/2B) after partition. Applications go to the Survey Dept / **ADLR** (Asst. Director of Land Records) via the **Mojini** system [K].
  - **Hadbast** (*ಹದ್ದುಬಸ್ತು*): official boundary-fixing survey, the first step in an **encroachment / boundary dispute** (*ಒತ್ತುವರಿ, ಬದು ಜಗಳ*) [K].
  - Stones are *ಗಡಿ ಕಲ್ಲು*. The raised field boundary is *ಬದು*.

### 1.4 Who can buy farmland: Karnataka Land Reforms Act, 1961
- The 2020 amendment (ordinance of **13 July 2020**, replaced by the Karnataka Land Reforms (Second Amendment) Act 2020, Act 56 of 2020) **omitted Sections 79A, 79B and 79C** [V]. Before that:
  - 79A barred people with non-farm income above ₹25 lakh/yr;
  - 79B barred companies and institutions;
  - 79C was the enforcement section.
- Now **non-agriculturists, including Bengaluru IT professionals, can buy agricultural land** [V]. Critics warned this would feed land banks and aggregators [V].
- **Still restricted** [K]:
  - **Ceiling limits** under s.63 still apply.
  - **Granted land** (*ಸರ್ಕಾರಿ ಮಂಜೂರು / ದರಖಾಸ್ತು*) to SC/ST grantees falls under the **PTCL Act 1978**, and transfer needs government permission.
  - **NRIs/OCIs cannot buy agricultural land in India** under FEMA (Non-Debt Instrument) Rules 2019, **but can inherit it** [K]. This is a good plot point if one sibling is abroad: they can inherit, but cannot buy out a sister's share.
- **Conversion to non-agricultural use** (*ಭೂ ಪರಿವರ್ತನೆ / ಕನ್ವರ್ಷನ್*, "DC conversion"):
  - The Karnataka Land Revenue (Amendment) Act 2023 changed **s.95**, effective 27-07-2023. The DC must decide within about **30 days**, or conversion is **deemed** granted on payment of the fee and an affidavit [V].
  - The 2025 Amendment Rules (notified Sept or Dec 2025; sources differ [?]) and a **Feb 2026 circular** further simplified the process [V/?].
  - Land within a local planning authority's master plan must match the zoning [K].

### 1.5 Lok Adalat and mediation
- The **Legal Services Authorities Act 1987, s.21**: a Lok Adalat award is **deemed a civil-court decree**, it is **final with no appeal**, and the **court fee is refunded** [K].
  - Run by **KSLSA** and the District Legal Services Authority (DLSA) Mandya, and at taluk level by the **Taluk Legal Services Committee**, Maddur [K].
- **2026 National Lok Adalats** [V, conflicting dates]:
  - 14 March 2026;
  - Karnataka 2nd sitting on **11 July 2026**, per the KSLSA notice;
  - September (12 Sept nationally; Karnataka moved to 19 Sept per one source);
  - a December sitting is expected but not confirmed [?].
- **Court-referred mediation** works under **s.89 CPC** and the **Mediation Act 2023** [K]. District courts have mediation centres; Mandya's is assumed [?]. A partition suit "referred to mediation" or settled at a Lok Adalat with a compromise decree is very realistic.
- **Story suggestion:** the siblings settle at a Lok Adalat. One sibling keeps farming the wetland and pays the others in instalments, the sister gets a dryland plot plus cash, and the court fee is refunded.

### 1.6 Money, loans, schemes
- **Crop loans** come through the **PACS / VSSN** (*ವ್ಯವಸಾಯ ಸೇವಾ ಸಹಕಾರ ಸಂಘ*) and the DCC Bank Mandya, or commercial banks via **KCC** (Kisan Credit Card) [K].
  - Karnataka gives **interest-free short-term co-op crop loans up to ₹5 lakh**, raised from ₹3 lakh in the 2023 budget, and medium/long-term loans at 3% up to ₹15 lakh [V].
  - Interest on **overdue co-op loans** was waived in 2024 [V].
  - The last big **crop-loan waivers** were 2017 (up to ₹50,000) and 2018 (Kumaraswamy, up to ₹2 lakh) [V/K]. No confirmed Karnataka waiver in 2025–26 [?].
  - The dead grandfather's outstanding PACS loan passes to the land and the heirs. This is a realistic complication.
- **PM-KISAN** pays ₹6,000/yr in three ₹2,000 instalments via DBT; land must be in the farmer's name and e-KYC is required [V].
  - The 23rd instalment was released on 20 June 2026 to about 9.4 crore farmers [V]. The 24th was announced for 30 Oct 2026 [V].
  - Dead owner's name on the RTC → heirs cannot get PM-Kisan until pauti is done. This is a strong motivation for mutation.
- **Crop insurance:** PMFBY [K].

## 2. Farming reality in Mandya

- **Sugarcane (*ಕಬ್ಬು*)** is Mandya's identity ("ಸಕ್ಕರೆ ನಾಡು", sugar land).
  - **FRP 2025-26** is **₹355/quintal** (₹3,550/tonne) at 10.25% recovery, up 4.41% from ₹340. Each 0.1% of recovery adds or cuts ₹3.46/qtl, with a floor of ₹329.05/qtl below 9.5% recovery. It applies from **1 Oct 2025** (CCEA, 30 Apr 2025) [V].
  - **Nov 2025 Karnataka protests:** the agitation was centred in Belagavi, Bagalkot and Mudhol, but Mandya was also mentioned. CM Siddaramaiah fixed **₹3,300/tonne** at 11.25% recovery (₹3,250 from the factory plus ₹50 from the state), or ₹3,200 at 10.25% [V]. In Mudhol, factories agreed to pay ₹3,300 regardless of recovery (14 Nov 2025) [V]. Whether Mandya mills paid this is unconfirmed [?]. The Karnataka figure is generally understood as the net price after harvest & transport deduction [?].
  - Mills legally must pay within **14 days**, with interest after that (Sugarcane Control Order 1966) [K]. In practice delays are common: Mandya's five factories once owed ₹230 crore [V, older].
  - **Mysugar** has a history of breakdowns:
    - 2024-25 crushing started 2 Aug, then halted by technical glitches [V];
    - ₹52 crore electricity dues to CESC (May 2024) [V];
    - Crushing for **2026-27** was launched by boiler ignition [V, DH, 2026].
  - Cane is a 12–14 month crop. The ratoon (second-year) crop is *ಕೂಳೆ ಕಬ್ಬು*. Harvest needs gangs of cutters, about **850–1,000 man-hours per acre** [V, older]. Harvesters (about ₹1 crore each) are hired, not owned [V].
  - **Jaggery (*ಬೆಲ್ಲ*) from *ಆಲೆಮನೆ***: production fell about 30% in some years. Units face labour shortage, high costs and no "industry" status [V, DH Feb 2025].
- **Paddy (*ಭತ್ತ*)**: canal-irrigated kharif crop, transplanted July–Aug and harvested Nov–Dec [K].
  - **KRS dam** (Krishnaraja Sagara) feeds the **Visvesvaraya Canal (VC)**, which commands about 1.96 lakh acres [V].
  - **2025:** KRS crossed 100 ft on **30 May 2025**, the first June filling in decades. The CM offered *ಬಾಗಿನ* [V]. Water was released from July 2025, then intermittently and in rotation (*ಕಟ್ಟು ಪದ್ಧತಿ*). Water to standing crops was extended into May 2026 [V].
  - Farmers complained of late water and canal "modernisation" closures. Ex-MLA Srikantaiah claimed paddy yields fell about 30% (a political claim) [V].
  - The **Cauvery dispute with Tamil Nadu** is a constant: **Aug 2026** saw protests over releases to TN [V]. **2023** was a drought year with big Mandya protests [K].
- **Ragi (*ರಾಗಿ*)**: rain-fed dryland crop, sown July–Aug and harvested Nov–Dec. It is the staple: *ರಾಗಿ ಮುದ್ದೆ* [K]. It is often intercropped with *ಅವರೆ* (field bean) and *ತೊಗರಿ* [K].
- **Other income** [K]: coconut, mulberry and **sericulture** (Maddur/Malavalli, with the Ramanagara cocoon market nearby), vegetables, and dairy.
- **Water:** **borewells** (*ಕೊಳವೆ ಬಾವಿ*) go deeper in drought years. Parts of Maddur taluk can't grow cane for lack of water [V]. Farm electricity is free but irregular; 3-phase power comes for only a few hours [K].
- **Labour and mechanisation:** labour shortage is acute, and tractors, power tillers, paddy transplanters and harvesters are rented [V/K]. Daily wage around ₹500–700 [?].
- **Debt and distress:** Mandya, with its irrigated cane belt, has repeatedly ranked high for farmer suicides. One example is the Hatna Bevukallu couple (crop loss, debt) [V, DH, date ?]. The usual causes are private moneylenders, cane price crashes and delayed mill payments [V].
  - **Handle with care in the lesson:** mention it, but do not dramatise.
- **Youth migration:** young people from the village go to Bengaluru (cabs, factories, IT and the service sector). Villages have many elderly people, and "*ಹುಡುಗೀರು ರೈತರ ಹುಡುಗನ್ನ ಮದುವೆ ಆಗಲ್ಲ*" (girls won't marry farmer boys) is a well-known Mandya social complaint, reported widely and even the subject of padayatras [K].
- **Land prices** [?, asking prices only]:
  - **Interior Maddur taluk** farmland lists at about **₹40–75 lakh/acre** (1acre.in, Maddur–Mandya, accessed Nov 2026; e.g. 1 acre 1 gunta at Dundanahalli).
  - **Highway/expressway frontage** and areas near Maddur town or the Channapatna/Ramanagara side ask several crores per acre. Wider corridor listings run ₹2.4–12 crore/acre [V, listings].
  - The **guidance value** (government rate) is far below market [V, analogous Bengaluru data].
  - Brokers sell "farm plots" and "gunta"-sized pieces to city buyers [K]. Story-friendly figure: a broker offers ₹55 lakh/acre for interior wetland, and ₹1.2 crore if converted and near the service road.

## 3. Village life

- **Gram Panchayat** (*ಗ್ರಾಮ ಪಂಚಾಯಿತಿ*, the elected body) [K]:
  - Officials: **Adhyaksha / Upadhyaksha** (president / vice-president) and the **PDO** (Panchayat Development Officer).
  - It holds the **Grama Sabha**, issues *ಇ-ಸ್ವತ್ತು* (e-Swathu) property records for village house sites (not farmland), and runs MGNREGA work.
  - It does **not** decide land inheritance.
- **Elders' panchayati** (*ಊರ ಪಂಚಾಯ್ತಿ, ಯಜಮಾನರು, ನ್ಯಾಯ ಪಂಚಾಯ್ತಿ*): an informal meeting at the temple or the *ಅರಳಿ ಕಟ್ಟೆ* (peepal-tree platform). The elders propose a "fair" division, often favouring the son who stayed and farmed. It has no legal force, but huge social weight [K].
- **Caste and community (respectful framing)** [K]:
  - Mandya is a **Vokkaliga** (*ಒಕ್ಕಲಿಗ*, title *ಗೌಡ*) majority district. There are also significant Dalit (SC), Kuruba, Muslim, Lingayat and other communities.
  - Writing guidance:
    - Show it through who sits where at the panchayati and who owns versus works land.
    - Avoid slurs, and avoid making any caste the villain.
    - Dalit families' granted lands under the PTCL rules are a real, sensitive issue.
- **Temple jatre** [K unless marked]:
  - Each village has a *ಗ್ರಾಮ ದೇವತೆ* (Maramma, Pattaladamma, Chowdeshwari, Bhairaveshwara…) and an annual **jatre**.
  - **Rathotsava / *ತೇರು***: the decorated wooden chariot is pulled with thick ropes by the crowd. Devotees throw **bananas and *ದವನ* (davana)** at the chariot, a strong Old Mysore custom.
  - Other features: *ಕೊಂಡೋತ್ಸವ* (fire-walk, common in Mandya/Mysuru goddess jatres), *ಹರಕೆ* (vows), *ಮುಡಿ* (tonsure), *ಪಂಜಿನ ಸೇವೆ*, *ಉತ್ಸವ ಮೂರ್ತಿ* in a *ಪಲ್ಲಕ್ಕಿ*, plus *ತಮಟೆ*, *ಡೊಳ್ಳು*, *ವೀರಗಾಸೆ*, and **ಪೂಜಾ ಕುಣಿತ** (a Mandya-region folk dance with a decorated pole balanced on the head).
  - **Fair stalls:** bangles (*ಬಳೆ*), *ಬೆಂಡು ಬತ್ತಾಸು*, *ಕಡ್ಲೆಪುರಿ*, *ಜಿಲೇಬಿ*, toys, a giant wheel; sometimes a cattle fair (*ದನಗಳ ಜಾತ್ರೆ*).
  - **Families return from Bengaluru for the jatre.** This makes it a natural venue for reconciliation, or for a fight.
  - The Maddur Ugra Narasimha temple lists a Brahma Rathotsava [?]. A *ಮದ್ದೂರಮ್ಮ ಜಾತ್ರೆ* in Maddur town is also plausible [?]. **Safer for the story: invent a fictional village goddess jatre.**
- **Food** [K]:
  - Staples: *ರಾಗಿ ಮುದ್ದೆ* with *ಬಸ್ಸಾರು* (a saaru made from the drained water of cooked greens/dal, with the greens as *ಪಲ್ಯ*), *ಸೊಪ್ಪಿನ ಸಾರು*, *ಉಪ್ಸಾರು*.
  - Festive and guest dishes: *ನಾಟಿ ಕೋಳಿ ಸಾರು* (country-chicken curry). Jatre feasts often include *ಬಾಡೂಟ* (meat meal) in many families.
  - Seasonal and local: *ಅವರೆಕಾಳು* (Dec–Feb season), *ಮದ್ದೂರು ವಡೆ*, *ಕಬ್ಬಿನ ಹಾಲು*, *ಬೆಲ್ಲ*, *ಎಳನೀರು*.
- **Sankranti (Jan)** [V/K]:
  - *ಎಳ್ಳು-ಬೆಲ್ಲ* (sesame, jaggery, copra, groundnut, fried gram) and *ಸಕ್ಕರೆ ಅಚ್ಚು* are exchanged: "*ಎಳ್ಳು ಬೆಲ್ಲ ತಿಂದು ಒಳ್ಳೆ ಮಾತಾಡಿ*" (eat sesame and jaggery and speak sweetly). This is a perfect line for a sibling reconciliation scene.
  - **Cattle are washed, horns painted, and made to jump over burning hay: *ಕಿಚ್ಚು ಹಾಯಿಸುವುದು***. Mandya injuries were reported on 15 Jan 2025 [V], and the ritual is criticised on animal-welfare grounds [V].
  - Also *ಹುಗ್ಗಿ / ಪೊಂಗಲ್* [K].
- **Other festivals:** Ugadi, Gowri-Ganesha, Dasara (Mysuru is nearby), Deepavali, *ಕಾರ್ತೀಕ* [K].

### 4.7 Useful phrases (story-ready)
- ತಾತನ ಹೆಸರಲ್ಲೇ ಇನ್ನೂ ಪಹಣಿ ಇದೆ. *tātana hesaralle innū pahaṇi ide.* "The RTC is still in Grandfather's name."
- ಪೌತಿ ಖಾತೆಗೆ ಅರ್ಜಿ ಹಾಕ್ಬೇಕು. *pauti khātege arji hākbēku.* "We must apply for inheritance mutation."
- ವಂಶವೃಕ್ಷದಲ್ಲಿ ಅಕ್ಕನ ಹೆಸರು ಬಿಟ್ಟು ಹೋಗಿದೆ. "Akka's name has been left out of the family tree."
- ಹೆಣ್ಣು ಮಕ್ಕಳಿಗೂ ಸಮ ಪಾಲು ಇದೆ, ಕಾನೂನು ಹಾಗೇ ಹೇಳುತ್ತೆ. "Daughters have an equal share too; that's what the law says."
- ನಾನು ಹಕ್ಕು ಬಿಡುಗಡೆ ಪತ್ರಕ್ಕೆ ಸಹಿ ಹಾಕಲ್ಲ. "I won't sign the release deed."
- ಕೇಸ್ ಹಾಕಿದ್ರೆ ಹತ್ತು ವರ್ಷ ಆದ್ರೂ ಮುಗಿಯಲ್ಲ. "If we file a case it won't finish even in ten years."
- ಲೋಕ ಅದಾಲತ್‌ನಲ್ಲಿ ರಾಜಿ ಮಾಡ್ಕೊಳ್ಳೋಣ. "Let's settle at the Lok Adalat."
- ಭೂಮಿ ಮಾರಿದ್ರೆ ದುಡ್ಡು ಹೋಗುತ್ತೆ, ಭೂಮಿ ವಾಪಸ್ ಬರಲ್ಲ. "Sell the land and the money goes; the land never comes back." [invented line, not a fixed proverb]
- Real proverbs/slogans:
  - ಉಳುವವನೇ ಹೊಲದೊಡೆಯ (*uḷuvavanē holadoḍeya*, "the tiller is the owner of the field"; the 1970s land-reform slogan of the Devaraj Urs era) [K].
  - ಕೂಡಿ ಬಾಳಿದರೆ ಸ್ವರ್ಗ ಸುಖ ("living together is heavenly bliss") [K].
  - ಎಳ್ಳು ಬೆಲ್ಲ ತಿಂದು ಒಳ್ಳೆ ಮಾತಾಡಿ ("eat sesame and jaggery and speak sweetly") [K].

## 5. Mandya / Old Mysore rural speech: features and registers

**Sources:** Star of Mysore (Dr B. Shakira Jabeen) confirms "Mandya Kannada" is a distinct variety differing from standard Kannada in tone, accent and vocabulary [V]. The specific features below come from background knowledge of Old Mysore colloquial Kannada [K]. Native-speaker review is recommended.

### 5.1 Features
| Feature | Standard | Mandya/Old Mysore colloquial | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| Vocative **-ಲಾ / -ಲೇ** (male intimate) | ಬಾ, ಏನೋ | **ಬಾರ್ಲಾ, ಏನ್ಲಾ, ಹೋಗ್ಲಾ, ಯಾಕ್ಲಾ** | Used only between male peers/close friends, or from elder to younger. Rude to an elder or stranger. **ಲೇ** can also address women among intimates. |
| Emphatic tag **ಕಣ** | ಹೌದು | **ಹೌದು ಕಣ, ಬಂದೆ ಕಣ್ಲಾ, ಆಯ್ತು ಕಣಪ್ಪ, ಇಲ್ಲ ಕಣಮ್ಮ, ಸರಿ ಕಣ್ರೀ** | The tag declines with the addressee: ಕಣ್ಲಾ (buddy), ಕಣೇ (girl/woman, intimate), ಕಣಪ್ಪ (man, warm), ಕಣಮ್ಮ (woman, warm), ಕಣ್ರೀ (respectful). The signature marker of South Karnataka speech. |
| **ಕೊಂಡು → ಕಂಡು / ಕೊಳ್ಳು → ಕಳ್ಳು** | ತೆಗೆದುಕೊಂಡು, ಕೂತುಕೊಳ್ಳಿ | **ತಗಂಡು, ಮಾಡ್ಕಂಡು, ಕುತ್ಕಳಿ / ಕುಂತ್ಕಳಿ, ತಕ್ಕಳಿ** | Very characteristic. |
| **i → u** in some verbs | ಬಿಡು, ಬಿಟ್ಟು | **ಬುಡು, ಬುಟ್ಟು** ("ಬುಟ್ಬುಡು", leave it) | Marked rural/Mandya, much imitated in films. Use sparingly. |
| **ಮಡಗು** for "put/keep" | ಇಡು | **ಮಡಗು / ಮಡಿಕ್ಕೋ** | Old Mysore word. |
| Present continuous contraction | ಹೋಗುತ್ತಾ ಇದ್ದೇನೆ | **ಹೋಯ್ತಾ ಇದ್ದೀನಿ / ಹೋಯ್ತಿದೀನಿ, ಬತ್ತಾ ಇದ್ದೀನಿ** | ಬರ್ತೀನಿ → **ಬತ್ತೀನಿ** (r-loss before t) is common rurally. |
| **ಇದೆ → ಅದೆ / ಐತೆ** | ನೀರು ಇದೆ | **ನೀರ್ ಅದೆ / ಐತೆ** | [?] ಅದೆ is heard around Mysuru/Mandya; ಐತೆ is more Tumakuru/Bengaluru-rural/North. Keep ಇದೆ for most characters. |
| **h-dropping** (some speakers) | ಹಾಲು, ಹೋಗು, ಹುಡುಗ | **ಆಲು, ಓಗು, ಉಡುಗ** | [?] Socially marked. Don't give it to every villager. |
| Address terms | | **ಮಗಾ / ಮಗ** (lit. "son", used to friends), **ಅಣ್ಣ** (any older man), **ಯಜಮಾನ್ರೇ, ಸ್ವಾಮಿ, ಗೌಡ್ರೇ** (respectful), **ಅವ್ವ** (mother, any older woman) | |
| Discourse fillers | | **ಅಂಗಂದ್ರೆ?** (meaning?), **ಅದೇನೋ ಅಂತಾರಲ್ಲ**, **ಅಲ್ವಾ ಮತ್ತೆ**, **ಏನ್ ಸಮಾಚಾರ?**, **ಊಟ ಆಯ್ತಾ?** (greeting) | ಹಂಗೆ / ಅಂಗೆ for ಹಾಗೆ; ಇಂಗೆ for ಹೀಗೆ. |

### 5.2 Sample lines
- **Farmer brother (stayed in village) to his younger brother:** ಏನ್ಲಾ, ಬೆಂಗ್ಳೂರಲ್ಲಿ ಕುಂತ್ಕಂಡು ಪಾಲು ಕೇಳ್ತೀಯ? ಈ ಜಮೀನ್ನ ಇಪ್ಪತ್ತು ವರ್ಷ ನಾನು ನೋಡ್ಕಂಡಿದ್ದೀನಿ ಕಣ್ಲಾ.
  - "So, sitting in Bengaluru you ask for a share? I've looked after this land for twenty years, man."
- **Mother:** ಜಗಳ ಬುಟ್ಬುಡ್ರಪ್ಪ, ನಿಮ್ಮಪ್ಪ ಇದ್ದಿದ್ರೆ ಇದನ್ನ ನೋಡಿ ಎಷ್ಟು ನೊಂದ್ಕಂತಿದ್ರು.
  - "Drop the fight, boys; if your father were alive, how hurt he'd be."
- **Elder at the panchayati:** ನೋಡಿ, ಹಿರಿಯರು ಸಂಪಾದ್ಸಿದ್ ಆಸ್ತಿ. ಎಲ್ರಿಗೂ ಸಮನಾಗಿ ಹಂಚ್ಕಳಿ. ಹೆಣ್ಮಗಳಿಗೂ ಒಂದ್ ಪಾಲು ಕೊಡ್ಬೇಕು, ಈಗ ಕಾನೂನೇ ಹಂಗೈತೆ.
  - "Look, it's property the elders earned. Share it equally. The daughter must get a share too; that's the law now."
  - Elders mix proverbs, slow measured speech and ಸ್ವಾಮಿ/ಅಪ್ಪ address.
- **Village Accountant (VAO):** polite-bureaucratic, heavy on documents, with English loans.
  - ಮರಣ ಪ್ರಮಾಣ ಪತ್ರ, ವಂಶವೃಕ್ಷ, ಎಲ್ಲಾ ವಾರಸುದಾರರ ಆಧಾರ್ ಕಾಪಿ ತಗಂಬನ್ನಿ. ಸ್ಥಳ ಮಹಜರ್ ಮಾಡಿ RIಗೆ ಕಳಿಸ್ತೀನಿ. ಸರ್ವರ್ ಡೌನ್ ಇದೆ, ನಾಳೆ ಬನ್ನಿ.
  - "Bring the death certificate, family tree and Aadhaar copies of all heirs. I'll do the spot mahazar and send it to the RI. The server's down; come tomorrow."
- **Tahsildar (formal, standard Kannada):** ಆಕ್ಷೇಪಣೆ ಬಂದಿರುವುದರಿಂದ ಇದು ವಿವಾದಿತ ಪ್ರಕರಣ. ಎರಡೂ ಕಡೆಯವರು ಮುಂದಿನ ವಿಚಾರಣೆಗೆ ದಾಖಲೆಗಳೊಂದಿಗೆ ಹಾಜರಾಗಬೇಕು.
  - "Since an objection has been received, this is a disputed case. Both parties must appear with documents at the next hearing."
- **Lawyer (Kannada-English code-mixing, taluk-court style):** ನೋಡಿ ಸಾರ್, ಮೊದ್ಲು partition suit file ಮಾಡಿ temporary injunction ತಗೋಬೇಕು, ಇಲ್ಲಾಂದ್ರೆ ನಿಮ್ಮಣ್ಣ ಮಾರಿಬಿಡ್ತಾರೆ. Preliminary decree ಬರೋಕೆ ಕನಿಷ್ಠ ಮೂರು-ನಾಲ್ಕು ವರ್ಷ ಆಗುತ್ತೆ. ರಾಜಿ ಆದ್ರೆ ಒಳ್ಳೇದು.
  - "First file the partition suit and get a temporary injunction, or your brother will sell. A preliminary decree takes at least 3–4 years. A compromise is better."
- **Lok Adalat conciliator (retired judge, gentle):** ಎಲ್ಲರಿಗೂ ಒಪ್ಪಿಗೆ ಆಗೋ ಹಾಗೆ ಒಂದು ದಾರಿ ಹುಡುಕೋಣ. ಇಲ್ಲಿ ಆದ ತೀರ್ಮಾನಕ್ಕೆ ಮೇಲ್ಮನವಿ ಇಲ್ಲ, ಕೋರ್ಟ್ ಫೀಸ್ ಕೂಡ ವಾಪಸ್ ಸಿಗುತ್ತೆ.
  - "Let's find a way acceptable to all. There's no appeal against a decision here, and you get the court fee back."
- **Bengaluru-raised grandchild (learner's proxy):** standard Kannada with English. ನಂಗೆ ಹಳ್ಳಿ ಕನ್ನಡ ಪೂರ್ತಿ ಅರ್ಥ ಆಗಲ್ಲ, ಸ್ವಲ್ಪ ನಿಧಾನವಾಗಿ ಹೇಳಿ.
  - "I don't fully understand village Kannada; please say it slowly."

### 5.3 Avoiding caricature
- Mandya speech is often played for comedy in Kannada films (the "ಬುಡ್ಲಾ / ಮಗಾ" stereotype of the loud, rough Mandya man). Give villagers a full range: dignified elders, a literate sister who knows the 2005 amendment, and a farmer who is articulate about FRP and canal schedules.
- Use **-ಲಾ** only where the relationship justifies it, such as brothers, boyhood friends, or an elder scolding a younger man. Never use it from a younger person to an elder.
- Keep dialect at the level of **markers** (ಕಣ, ಕಂಡು, ಬುಡು, ಬತ್ತೀನಿ) rather than phonetic spelling of every word, so the text stays readable for learners. Give a standard-Kannada gloss the first time each form appears.
- Women's speech: older women use ಅವ್ವ/ಕಣವ್ವ/ಕಣಮ್ಮ forms and often speak strongly and directly. Don't write them as silent.
- No caste slurs, and no ಮುಂಡೇದು-type abuse in learner material.

## 6. Suggested lesson arc (optional)
1. Funeral and 11th-day rites in the village. Thatha's RTC is still in his own name, and the PM-Kisan money has stopped.
2. Nadakacheri: family-tree certificate (*ವಂಶವೃಕ್ಷ*). The sister's name is "forgotten".
3. Elders' panchayati at the *ಅರಳಿ ಕಟ್ಟೆ*. The elder brother wants everything because he farmed it; the sister cites the 2005 law.
4. A Bengaluru broker offers ₹55 lakh/acre, more after conversion. Since 2020, outsiders can buy farmland.
5. Partition suit filed in Maddur and an injunction granted. Years loom.
6. Farming season: the cane goes to the mill but payment is late; paddy waits on the VC canal; a borewell fails.
7. Jatre: chariot pulling and davana. The family meets; Sankranti *ಎಳ್ಳು-ಬೆಲ್ಲ* and "speak sweetly".
8. Lok Adalat compromise; registered partition deed; podi/hissa; mutation done.
9. The decision: one sibling keeps farming (dairy plus cane), and one sells a small dryland portion. Open ending.
