## 5. Law (for the divorce & cheque-bounce/consumer subplots)

**Mutual-consent divorce (Hindu Marriage Act s.13B)** [V/GK]
- File jointly after **≥1 year living separately** (13B(1)) → first motion → **6-month cooling-off** → second motion (within 18 months) → decree.
- **Amardeep Singh v. Harveen Kaur (SC, 2017)**: 6-month period is *directory*, can be waived (separation ≥1.5 yrs incl. the year, mediation failed, settlement complete). **Shilpa Sailesh v. Varun Sreenivasan (SC Constitution Bench, May 2023)**: SC can dissolve under Art. 142 for irretrievable breakdown. 2025 Delhi HC: both periods can be waived but "not merely for the asking". [V for law; Bengaluru-specific timeline not found]
- Realistic: Bengaluru family courts (Shantinagar) — uncontested mutual divorce typically ~6–8 months; with waiver can be faster. [?] practitioner estimate, not verified.
- Settlement issues: alimony lump sum, stridhan return, custody — all must be in the joint petition.

**Cheque bounce (Negotiable Instruments Act s.138)** [V]
- Present cheque within 3 months → dishonour memo → **legal notice within 30 days** → drawer has **15 days** to pay → complaint within 1 month after that. Punishment up to 2 yrs and/or fine up to 2× cheque amount; offence compoundable.
- **Sanjabij Tari v. Kishore S. Borcar (SC, 25 Sep 2025, 2025 INSC 1158)**: nationwide guidelines — dasti + electronic summons, accused can pay at first stage, **reduced compounding costs** vs Damodar Prabhu (2010) to encourage settlement; courts told to implement by 1 Nov 2025; cash loans ≥₹20k still enforceable debt despite IT Act s.269SS. [V] (exact new % not retrieved)

**Consumer commission** [GK — Consumer Protection Act 2019 + Jurisdiction Rules 2021]
- District Commission: claims up to ₹50 lakh; State: ₹50 lakh–₹2 crore; National: above ₹2 crore. File online via **e-Daakhil**; no lawyer needed; complaint within 2 years of cause of action. Typical disputes: builder delay, car dealer, insurance claim, airline refunds.

## 6. Road trip realities (route as written: Bengaluru → Chitradurga → Hampi → Hubballi → Gokarna)

Distances/times are [GK] approximations (check Maps):
- **Bengaluru → Chitradurga**: ~200 km on **NH-48** (Tumakuru, Sira, Hiriyur), 3–3.5 h. Tolls on NH-48 near Nelamangala revised 22 Jul 2026 [V, NewsBytes/DH]. FASTag annual pass (₹3,000/200 trips, from 15 Aug 2025) [GK].
- **Chitradurga fort** ("Kallina Kote", seven rings; Onake Obavva who fought Hyder Ali's soldiers with a pestle (onake) — Kannada folk heroine; Obavvana Kindi) [GK].
- **Chitradurga → Hosapete/Hampi**: ~140 km on NH-50 (old NH-13), ~2.5–3 h [?]. NB **Davanagere is NOT on this leg** — it's on NH-48 between Chitradurga and Hubballi. For benne dose, either detour (Chitradurga→Davanagere ~60 km) or stop on Hampi→Hubballi? (no — that leg goes via Koppal/Gadag). Writer's choice: a detour argument is a nice story beat.
- **Hampi**: UNESCO World Heritage Site since 1986 [GK]; Virupaksha temple (living temple), Vittala temple stone chariot (on ₹50 note), Hemakuta hill sunsets, Matanga hill sunrise, boulder landscape, coracle rides on the Tungabhadra, Anegundi/"hippie island" across river, Sanapur lake [GK/V].
- **Salu mantapa (pavilion row) near Virupaksha partially collapsed after rain, 21 May 2024**; ASI funding woes for restoration [V] (DH, Deccan Chronicle, TNM May 2024).
- **Hampi Utsava 2026 was 13–15 Feb 2026** (1,000-drone show, helicopter rides); CM announced it will be held **annually 3–5 November** going forward; whether a Nov 2026 edition happened = [?] (conflicting). Safe for the story: "Utsava just happened / we missed it".
- **Tungabhadra dam (Hosapete)**: **Crest gate No. 19 washed away night of 10 Aug 2024**; temporary stop-log gate; all **33 crest gates replaced** (Ahmedabad contractor, ~₹52 cr); **inaugurated 25 June 2026 by CM DKS with Telangana & AP CMs** [V] (DH, ThePrint, Siasat). Farmers lost a crop season (dam couldn't hold full storage in 2025) [V-ish, Deccan Chronicle].
- **Hampi → Hubballi**: ~165 km via Koppal, Gadag (~3 h). **Hubballi–Dharwad** twin cities; Dharwad = Hindustani music (Bhimsen Joshi, Gangubai Hangal, Mallikarjun Mansur), Karnataka University, "Dharwad pedha" (Thakur/Babusingh Thakur pedha, GI tag) [GK].
- **Hubballi → Gokarna**: ~160 km via Yellapur–Ankola (NH-52/NH-63), Western Ghats stretch, 3–4 h; landslide/potholes in monsoon; Shirur landslide on NH-66 near Ankola **July 2024** (lorry driver Arjun's body found months later) [GK].
- **Gokarna**: Mahabaleshwara temple (Atmalinga, Ravana legend); beaches Kudle, **Om** (shaped like ॐ), Half Moon, Paradise. Swimming officially prohibited in places, drownings recur; lifeguards rescued several at Gokarna/Kudle after Murudeshwar's closure (Dec 2024) [V-ish, NIE via Magzter; Lonely Planet].
- **Murudeshwar**: giant Shiva statue (~123 ft) + temple gopura with lift; **beach closed Dec 2024 after 4 schoolgirls from Kolar drowned** [V partial — closure confirmed; numbers GK].
- **Yana**: karst limestone rock outcrops (Bhairaveshwara & Mohini shikhara), near Kumta/Sirsi, short forest walk; "Yana ge hodru ... " proverb? — the actual saying is **"Sokku iddare Yana, rokka iddare Gokarna"** (if you have arrogance/energy go to Yana, if you have money go to Gokarna) [GK, wording ?].
- Monsoon caveat: coast heavy rains Jun–Sep; story set in winter (Nov–Dec) = ideal season.
