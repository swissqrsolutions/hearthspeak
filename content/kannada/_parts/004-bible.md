# Lesson 4 — "ನಮ್ಮ ದರ್ಶಿನಿ" (Our darshini) — STORY BIBLE

A serious, realistic, continuous story of **at least 1 h 30 min (~780 spoken lines)**, B2 → B2+. Opening and running a small standing restaurant (darshini) in Jayanagar, Bengaluru, through one real year (Oct 2025 – Nov 2026) with its real 2026 crises. No silly lines; warmth and humour only where real people would have them.

## Hard rules (same as Lessons 2–3 — read the "Hard rules" in C:/code/language/content/kannada/_parts/002-bible.md and follow them)
- YAML items like the earlier lessons: `chapter`, `section` + `learn`, `scene`, `{ who, t, tr, en }`; all strings double-quoted; Kannada script correct; `tr` with macrons; natural English. **No English letters inside `t`** (write loans in Kannada script: ಜಿಎಸ್‌ಟಿ, ಲೈಸೆನ್ಸ್, ಪಾರ್ಸೆಲ್). Stage directions only in `en`.
- **Section titles without emoji.**
- Register: family/staff talk = natural Bengaluru Kannada (+ coastal Udupi/Kundapura colour for the cook, North-Karnataka colour for one helper, used with dignity); officials, landlord, bank = polite professional Kannada with the English terms people really use (trade licence, FSSAI, GST, MUDRA, rent agreement, deposit, token, parcel, UPI).
- **Reuse Lessons 1–3 vocabulary** (C:/code/language/research_notes/known_vocab_after_L2.txt plus Lesson 3) and introduce new words in context (business, money, food & kitchen, licences, staff, customers, negotiation).

- Grammar to stress: business conditionals and hypotheticals (ಲಾಭ ಬಂದ್ರೆ…, ನಷ್ಟ ಆದ್ರೂ…), causatives (ಮಾಡಿಸು, ಕಟ್ಟಿಸು), obligation & permission in rules, reported speech from officials, numbers/percentages/money, comparisons (ಚೈನ್‌ಗಿಂತ…), polite refusal and negotiation, motivating staff, apologising to customers.

## Chapters, beats, line budgets (≈ 780 spoken lines)
1. *The dream and the money* (~80, Oct 2025). Mahesh quits his supervisor job; Kavya's spreadsheet: setup ₹25–35 lakh estimate; savings, Amma's gold, Nitin's ₹8 lakh as partner (partnership deed, profit share), MUDRA Kishore/Tarun loan with Ms. Rashmi (limits, documents, CIBIL, collateral-free, EMI), family fears; proprietorship vs partnership vs LLP (CA Mr. Bhat).
2. *Finding the space* (~90, Nov 2025). Broker Prakash: three spaces (main road vs side lane, foot traffic near the bus stop and metro, school nearby, parking, water, drainage, exhaust chimney rules); Jayanagar 4th Block, 500 sq ft at ~₹200/sq ft/month, 10-month deposit; negotiation with old landlord Venkatesh Murthy (deposit down, 11-month vs 3-year lease, rent escalation 5%, lock-in, who pays civil work, his rules: no non-veg — it's a veg darshini anyway); registered lease.
3. *Licences* (~100, Dec 2025). One section each: GBA trade licence (online, fee, inspection, renewal penalties), FSSAI (new 2026 thresholds — registration vs state licence; hedge dates), GST (₹20 lakh threshold, 5% without ITC vs composition), Shops & Establishments, fire safety (when NOC needed — hedge), the 60% Kannada signboard rule; frustrations (portal down, a missing document) and positives (online tracking).
4. *Kitchen, menu and suppliers* (~85, Dec 2025–Jan 2026). Equipment (grinder, dosa tawa, steamer, coffee filter, chimney), commercial LPG connection & deposit, menu & pricing debate (idli-vada, masala dose, kesari bath, filter coffee — prices with cost per plate), suppliers: KR Market vegetables at dawn with Ramesh, Nandini milk, rice, oil; credit terms.
5. *Hiring the team* (~75, Jan 2026). Head cook Shankara from Kundapura (salary, his conditions, pride, recipes), helpers Basavaraj and Dev (wages, food & lodging, room, phones, Sunday off?), cashier Latha (UPI, token system); rules, hygiene, uniforms; Dev learning Kannada (lovely mirror of the learner).
6. *Opening day and the first months* (~85, 26 Jan 2026 — Republic Day opening). Puja, first customers, Sam among them; rush hour, token chaos, a complaint (cold sambar), apology & fix; cash vs UPI; first month's accounts; Amma's chutney.
7. *The gas crisis* (~70, Mar 2026). Commercial LPG stops (West Asia conflict): distributor Ashok, black-market offers refused, firewood/induction options, menu cut to five items, staff morale, customers' patience, losses.
8. *Wages and the apps* (~85, May–Aug 2026). The minimum-wage order (+~60%, under court challenge — hedge): staff meeting, honest numbers, price rise debate (idli price), customers' reactions; Swiggy/Zomato: Rohit's offer, commissions 20–30%, discounts, packaging, the restaurants' protest; decision.
9. *Inspection and competition* (~70, Sep–Oct 2026). Surprise health inspection with Mr. Gowda: real issues (drain, pest control record) and a hinted "adjustment" Mahesh politely refuses (asks for written notice and receipt); fixes; a famous chain café opens 200 m away — fear, then differentiation (filter coffee, regulars, Amma's recipes).
10. *One year* (~60, Nov 2026). Nandini milk +₹8/litre; one-year accounts with Kavya and Nitin: break-even reached? EMI, profit share; staff bonus; Shankara's offer to leave for a chain — honest talk; closing scene at the counter at 5 a.m.: Mahesh, Amma and the first customer of the second year.
