# Lesson 5 — "ತಾತನ ಜಮೀನು" (Grandfather's land) — STORY BIBLE

A serious, realistic, continuous story of **at least 1 h 30 min (~780 spoken lines)**, B2 → B2+/C1. A Bengaluru family's grandfather dies in his village near Maddur (Mandya district); his 6 acres of land divide three siblings, bring in a broker, a court case, a farming season, the village jatre and finally a Lok Adalat settlement. One real year: Dec 2025 – Dec 2026. Respectful, no caricature of village people; no silly lines.

## Chapters, beats, line budgets (≈ 780 spoken lines)
1. *The call* (~70, Dec 2025). Night call to Suresh in Bengaluru: Appa (Thatha, 74) died in the village. Drive to Maddur; the house, the body, rituals, relatives; condolence language; Avva's grief; first tension: Mahadeva's line "I'm the one who stayed".
2. *The eleventh day* (~70). Funeral rites and the 11th-day ceremony (ವೈಕುಂಠ ಸಮಾರಾಧನೆ / tithi), village food; discovery: the RTC (ಪಹಣಿ) is still in Thatha's name, PM-Kisan stopped, a cooperative crop loan in his name; Thatha's old diary.
3. *Papers* (~85, Jan 2026). Nadakacheri / Village Accountant Siddaraju: death certificate, family-tree certificate (ವಂಶವೃಕ್ಷ), pauti khata (mutation); Shobha's name "missing" from a draft list; Revenue Inspector; Tahsildar's hearing; delays, a hinted fee refused.
4. *The elders* (~85). Panchayati under the peepul (ಅರಳಿ ಕಟ್ಟೆ) with elder Thimmegowda: Mahadeva wants all the farmland because he farmed it for 20 years; Shobha cites the 2005 law and *Vineeta Sharma*; Suresh in between; Avva's wish; deep family history, old wounds; no agreement.
5. *The broker* (~75, Mar 2026). Broker Manjunath: ₹55 lakh/acre near the expressway, more after conversion; who can buy farmland since 2020; temptation vs. heritage; Rakshith's view; Meena's numbers; Mahadeva's anger.
6. *The case* (~85, Apr 2026). Shobha files a partition suit in Maddur through Advocate Nagendra; summons; Mahadeva gets an injunction; court fee, timelines ("decades"); the family stops speaking; Avva caught in the middle.
7. *The season* (~95, Jun–Nov 2026). Suresh spends weekends farming with Mahadeva: sugarcane (FRP ₹355/quintal vs state ₹3,300/tonne, Mysugar payment delays, field officer), paddy waiting on the VC canal from KRS, a failed borewell, labour shortage, hired harvester, dairy; brothers slowly reconcile; Rakshith learns.
8. *The jatre* (~70). Village temple festival: chariot pulling, devotees, fire-walking, pooja kunitha, food; Shobha comes for Avva; the three siblings meet; the priest; a quiet night talk between Avva and her children.
9. *Lok Adalat* (~75). With the conciliator: everyone speaks; compromise (Mahadeva keeps the wet land and the house to farm with dairy; Shobha takes her share in money from sale of a dryland portion; Suresh gives up his share to Avva's name); registered partition deed, podi/hissa, mutation; court fee refunded.
10. *Sankranti* (~70, Jan 2027 epilogue or Dec 2026). Ellu-bella ("eat ellu-bella and speak sweetly"), cattle decorated (fire-jumping ritual mentioned safely), the family together; Suresh's closing words to Rakshith about land, memory and family — serious and warm.
