Hosa mane — The new home

Sam, 28, from Switzerland, has a new IT job in Bengaluru. Instead of a lonely flat, Sam takes a room as a paying guest (PG) with the Rao family in Jayanagar. It's a busy house: Appa, Amma, Ajji, their son Kiran and their daughter Shwetha. Tonight is Sam's first evening there.
