Hosa mane — The new home

Sam, 28, from Switzerland, has a new IT job in Bengaluru and takes a room as a paying guest (PG) with the Rao family in Jayanagar: Appa, Amma, Ajji, their son Kiran and their daughter Shwetha. This is Sam's first weekend in the house, from Friday evening to Sunday night: food, family, the market, work life, neighbours, an arranged-marriage visit and a fight about dowry. The talk starts very simple and grows into full-speed native conversation.
